How to Prepare 9ja Party Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a famous West African one-pot dish, loved at parties, weddings and family meals. Long-grain rice is cooked in a rich, smoky sauce of tomatoes, peppers and onions until every grain is full of flavour. This recipe serves about six people and takes roughly one hour.

Recipe Overview for 9ja Party Jollof Rice

Prep time is 20 minutes and cooking time is 40 minutes. The secret to great Jollof is a well-fried pepper base and a gentle, steamy finish.

Ingredients for 9ja Party Jollof Rice

For the pepper base

  • 4 large ripe tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 1 Scotch bonnet pepper (adjust to taste)
  • 2 medium onions, divided
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste

For the rice

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 2 stock cubes
  • Salt to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the rice in cold water until the water runs mostly clear, then drain and set aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, Scotch bonnet and one onion until smooth.
  3. Pour the blended mixture into a pot and boil on medium heat until the water has reduced and the mixture is thick.
  4. Heat the oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot. Slice the second onion and sauté it until soft and golden.
  5. Stir in the tomato paste and fry for 3 to 5 minutes until it darkens.
  6. Add the reduced pepper mixture and fry for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring often, until the oil rises to the top and the sauce no longer tastes sour.
  7. Add the thyme, curry powder, bay leaves, stock cubes and salt. Stir well.
  8. Add the rice and the stock. Stir so the rice is fully coated, then check that the liquid just covers the rice.
  9. Cover the pot tightly with foil and a lid. Cook on low heat for 25 to 30 minutes without stirring.
  10. Turn off the heat, fluff the rice gently with a fork, and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Safety Notes and Warnings

Warning: Hot oil can splash and cause serious burns. Add the tomato paste and the blended pepper mixture slowly, and keep your face and hands away from the pot.

Important: Wash your hands with soap after handling the Scotch bonnet pepper, and never touch your eyes or face before doing so. Wear gloves if you have sensitive skin.

Food safety: Cooked rice should be served hot or cooled quickly. Do not leave rice at room temperature for more than two hours, because bacteria can grow and cause food poisoning. Reheat leftovers until steaming hot.

Cooking Terms Explained

Parboiled rice
Rice that has been partially boiled in its husk before milling. It stays firm and separate when cooked, which makes it ideal for Jollof.
Sauté
To cook food quickly in a small amount of hot oil, stirring often, until it softens or browns lightly.
Reduce
To boil a liquid until some of the water evaporates, leaving a thicker, more concentrated mixture.
Simmer
To cook gently in liquid just below boiling point, with only small bubbles rising.
Fluff
To loosen cooked rice with a fork so the grains separate and steam escapes.
